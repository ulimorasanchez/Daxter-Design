About Blip & Co.

The tiny animated universe of Blip, Doodle, and Nib — built one chaotic short at a time.

Meet the Crew

Blip is a hungry blob with endless optimism. Doodle is a skateboarding dog with zero fear and zero brakes. Nib is a sleepy bird‑cat who naps through most disasters.

Blip & Co. started as a small doodle series and grew into weekly animated shorts, stickers, and behind‑the‑scenes posts.

Origin Story

2024 — The First Doodle

Blip was sketched on a sticky note during a late-night animation session. The round shape stuck — literally.

2025 — Doodle Joins

A chaotic dog with a skateboard appeared in the margins of a storyboard. He immediately caused problems.

2026 — Nib Arrives

A sleepy hybrid creature was added to balance the chaos. It did not work. Nib sleeps through everything.
