✦ the person behind the blobs

Hi, I'm Mars.

I'm a one-person animation studio working out of a very cluttered apartment, making short cartoons about a small hungry blob named Blip, his skateboard-obsessed dog Doodle, and their perpetually unbothered bird-cat roommate Nib.

What started as a sketchbook doodle in 2023 turned into a weekly animated short, and eventually into a sticker shop, because apparently everyone wants Blip's unimpressed face on their water bottle. I don't blame them.

📖 origin story

Where the characters came from

2023

A blob shows up in a notebook margin

Blip started as a doodle drawn during a boring meeting — a round pink shape with a face that somehow had more personality than most cartoon leads. He stuck around.

2024

Doodle and Nib join the crew

Blip needed a rival and a straight man. Enter Doodle, a chaotic good dog-blob with zero spatial awareness, and Nib, a bird-cat who has never once looked impressed by anything.

2025

The first sticker sheet sells out in a day

A test batch of 50 "Blip Mid-Zoom" stickers, made for fun, sold out before I finished making the second batch. The shop was born out of pure demand.

2026

Weekly shorts, a real shop, and this website

Now: a new short almost every week, a growing sticker catalog, and one increasingly organized studio (a low bar, but we're clearing it).

🎯

Mission

Make small, funny, sincere stories that take thirty seconds to watch and somehow live in your head all week.

🎨

Style

Rounded shapes, big expressions, squash-and-stretch animation. Nothing on screen is ever perfectly still.

💌

Community first

Every character quirk in the show started as a comment or a sticker request. You're basically a co-writer at this point.
